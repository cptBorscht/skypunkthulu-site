#SideQuest 

Unlock the mysterious powers of the Bal Bal deck by identifying the power level of each card (putting them in correct order)

Bal Bal Rules:

Winner is the player with the biggest power difference between their cards.

Players place an agreed upon Ante.
There is a round where you can trade 1 card, then a round of betting.
A Dealer chip indicates who deals the deck.  The player to the Left of Dealer is the Better.
Only the Better may Raise the Ante.

Whoever is left shows their hand.  If nobody knows the rules, the winning cards will glow with eldritch light.

Those who know the cards in their power order, may draw Eldritch Magic boons from the deck.

***If you reveal too much to an uninitiated player, the deck will withdraw its power from you***
	- Conventionally, people stick to teaching 2 rules and the best and worst cards.  The deck seems fine with that
