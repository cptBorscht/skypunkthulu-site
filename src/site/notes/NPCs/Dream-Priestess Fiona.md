Half-Elf, Half-Dragonborn

Gave you tea to awaken latent Dreaming powers.  

She was a priestess in Bal, and though it is unclear if her origins were within the Twilight Temples, she became most widely known as the High Cleric there, before she chose to undergo a ceremony which put her to sleep to explore the Dreamlands.  Shanksworth was happy to let her sleep and sieze power.

She hid her true body with a sleeping double illusion, and has since left her Long Dream  to assist Elba. 

She told you that Marcus was here when the first Dreamlanders came to empty Dreamland.

She remains in Ventissa to help the local people resist the Eldritch Society's takeover, but has provided books and stabilized Elba's condition for the time being.
