Presumed Human

The Pirate Prince of Ventissa, a pragmatic but often mercurial man, Cirque de la Nuit hold a special place in his heart for their frankness with him, and for their ability to unite the people of Ventissa.  

Even though he started out considering you nuissances and riff-raff, he has since been impressed by your demonstrations of power and prowess.  And considers you something of a rival.
