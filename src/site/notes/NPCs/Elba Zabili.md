Human

A cherubim and sharp, if anxious, young woman.  She appears to have great powers, especially as a Dreamer, but she is scared of her powers, and there are too many that seek to abuse her and her gifts to their own aims.

Her early life is still somewhat mysterious, but it seems she came to be part of Montemerrat's estate as a ward in childhood.  Alonso eventually proposed, a surprise, given they had never been so romantic, or even very close.  Still she felt pressured to agree.  

Doc Furn (a Montemerrat) eventually began experiments, benign at first, but strange, uncomfortable and even painful by the time she decided to stow away aboard the Unpronounceable, and found herself traveling with Cirque de la Nuit.  Now the Montemerrats are after her, using the engagement as pretense.

Since traveling with the crew, she started glowing at night while she is in the Dreamlands, meaning she's doing things that are sure to have direct and real impacts in the waking world. Priestess Fiona has explained the mental exercises she needs to perform to stay in the waking world. 

An otherwise bright, helpful, and cheerful young lady, it is a shame the Fates have chosen her for such travails.
