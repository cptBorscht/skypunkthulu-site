A instrument-maker's stall that sells nothing you would want played at you.
Every piece here is a bard focus first and a weapon second: you attack with
your CHA modifier, you can burn a Bardic Inspiration die for damage, and a
NAT 1 opens the wild magic table. Each instrument comes two ways — the real
thing, which strikes psychic, and a pistol wearing its shape, which strikes
piercing.
