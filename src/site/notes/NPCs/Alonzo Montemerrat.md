Human

Son of [[NPCs/High Magister Miranda Montemerrat\|High Magister Miranda Montemerrat]], this diletante is known by Cirque de la Nuit to be a member of the Cult of Kundall, worshipping in forbidden rituals, a relic Dragon God named Kundall.  He has a full-back tattoo of the dragon god.  He is, or was, engaged to be wed to [[NPCs/Elba Zabili\|Elba Zabili]], but has never demonstrated more than passing interest in Elba.

His Uncle is [[NPCs/Doc Furn\|Doc Furn]].

He gives his dog a mimic cake to fight each year.
