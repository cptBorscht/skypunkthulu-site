Presumed Human

High Magister Miranda is sister to [[NPCs/Doc Furn\|Doc Furn]], and is one of the *highest* ranking members of the Eldritch Society.  Though you have not encountered her directly, you are by now aware of her by name, and have likely seen some images of her.  She is a meticulously put together woman, ambitious, and protects her brother's otherwise tabboo works in order to harness his intellect for her own aims.

Possibly a Dreamer, based on a sighting in the Dreamlands.  Quinn cannot be certain, as she was seen attempting to get a closer look at them.

Cirque de la Nuit has by now earned her full ire, and been identified as the _thieves_ who absconded with anything not nailed down at the Magister Prince Alonzo's birthday party (Session Prologue), as well as allies of the Pirate Rebellion in Bal.  Ventissa is not recognized by Miranda, nor the Eldritch Society.
