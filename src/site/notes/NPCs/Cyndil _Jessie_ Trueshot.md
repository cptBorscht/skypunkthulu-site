Elf (or unknown Half-Elf), the Trueshot bloodline may or may not be as perfectly Elven as stated.

Knows where Kovo buried her stash.

Got a longbow, eschewing guns, like all of the proud Elven Trueshot name.  Kovo knows some of Jessie's personal secrets, but otherwise she is tight-lipped about her past and future.  She is a former Merchants' Guild member, but seemingly no longer feels she can rely on their protection. 

The Celestial Courts are apparently after her, but not for any crimes logged anywhere; and no bounties exist on Jessies head.

Met on Spring Mai 6, 1223, on the Resort Skyland of Druun.

She *strongly* requested that you take her to Goa Zo, where you landed on Spring Mai 13, 1223.
